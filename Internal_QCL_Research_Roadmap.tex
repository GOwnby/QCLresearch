\begin{abstract}
\noindent
We present the revised and expanded six-paper theoretical and computational research program synthesized from our completed mathematical investigations in \textbf{Papers~I--III}. Phase~I of this program resolved three foundational bottlenecks in terahertz (THz) and mid-infrared (mid-IR) quantum cascade lasers (QCLs): (i)~the $261\,\mathrm{K}$ operating temperature ceiling imposed by tall-barrier interface-roughness (IFR) dephasing and over-resonant longitudinal-optical (LO) phonon extraction (\textbf{Paper~I}), (ii)~non-adiabatic extendon phase turbulence and multi-stack gain curvature in octave-spanning heterogeneous frequency combs (\textbf{Paper~II}), and (iii)~deterministic Argand-plane topological phase-kink addressing of dissipative soliton molecules and sub-picosecond pulse compression (\textbf{Paper~III}). Auditing the exact analytical theorems proved in Papers~I--III reveals three universal mathematical structures---exponential WKB boundary screening coupled with Schrieffer--Wolff continuum elimination, non-adiabatic Chapman--Enskog memory coupling stabilized by under-coupled non-Hermitian poles, and complex oscillatory tail potentials with broken spatial parity---that transcend III--V intersubband lasers. In \textbf{Phase~II (Papers~IV--VI)}, we push directly into these open frontiers across solid-state quantum information, non-Hermitian quantum optics, and nonequilibrium statistical mechanics: (\textbf{Paper~IV}) universal step-graded interface screening of valley-orbit dephasing in $\mathrm{Si/SiGe}$ spin qubits and two-level-system (TLS) / Bogoliubov quasiparticle poisoning in superconducting transmon and fluxonium qubits; (\textbf{Paper~V}) non-Hermitian Exceptional-Point ($\mathrm{EP}_2$) dispersion engineering and broadband Quantum Langevin twin-beam intensity squeezing in Kerr--Bloch and Josephson traveling-wave metamaterial combs; and (\textbf{Paper~VI}) non-reciprocal chiral dissipative soliton time crystals and sub-picosecond topological Ising spin-glass machines in driven nonlinear ring resonators.
\end{abstract}

\section{Cross-Disciplinary Mathematical Dualities Discovered in Papers I--III}

A critical examination of the proofs in Appendices~A--D of Papers~I--III shows that our results are governed by three general operator-theoretic mechanisms that extend far beyond semiconductor quantum cascade lasers:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Duality I: WKB Evanescent Boundary Screening and Schrieffer--Wolff Leakage Suppression (From Paper~I to Solid-State Spin \& Superconducting Qubits).}
    In Theorem~1 of Paper~I, the Ando--Unuma interface-roughness scattering rate across a heterointerface at $z = z_i$ is governed by the contact functional $\mathcal{I}_{\mathrm{int}} = \sum_i \Lambda_i^2 \eta_i^2 \delta V_i^2 |\Delta\psi^2(z_i)|^2$. Because the envelope wavefunction decays exponentially inside a barrier step as $|\psi(z_i + d_{\mathrm{buf}})|^2 = |\psi(z_i)|^2 e^{-2\bar{\kappa} d_{\mathrm{buf}}}$, splitting a single large potential step $\delta V_{\mathrm{core}}$ into an outer step $u\delta V_{\mathrm{core}}$ and an inner step $(1-u)\delta V_{\mathrm{core}}$ creates a convex quadratic functional in $u$ whose global minimum $\mathcal{R}^* = \xi/(1 + \xi e^{4\bar{\kappa} d_{\mathrm{buf}}}) < 1/2$ exponentially suppresses interfacial decoherence.
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textit{Isomorphism to $\mathrm{Si/SiGe}$ Spin-Valley Qubits:} In isotopically purified ${}^{28}\mathrm{Si}/\mathrm{Si}_{1-x}\mathrm{Ge}_x$ quantum dots, the coherence time $T_2^*$ of singlet-triplet and Loss--DiVincenzo spin qubits is bottlenecked by random variability in the conduction-band valley splitting $\Delta_{\mathrm{v}} = 2|\langle \psi_+ | V_{\mathrm{int}}(z) e^{-2i k_0 z} | \psi_- \rangle|$ caused by atomic-step roughness at the $\mathrm{Si}/\mathrm{Si}_{0.7}\mathrm{Ge}_{0.3}$ interface ($k_0 = 0.85 \times 2\pi/a_0$). By choosing the step-graded buffer thickness to be an integer multiple of the valley interference period, $d_{\mathrm{buf}} = m(\pi/k_0)$ ($m \in \mathbb{Z}^+$), the outer and inner steps interfere \textit{constructively} for the deterministic valley splitting $\Delta_{\mathrm{v}}$ while screening the incoherent disorder variance $\sigma_{\Delta_{\mathrm{v}}}^2 \propto \mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{IFR}}^* < 1/2$.
        \item \textit{Isomorphism to Superconducting Transmon/Fluxonium Qubits:} In superconducting $\mathrm{Al/AlO_x/Al}$ and $\mathrm{Nb/Si}$ quantum circuits, energy relaxation $T_1$ is limited by two mechanisms that mirror Paper~I's Theorem~1 (interfacial roughness) and Appendix~C (Schrieffer--Wolff continuum escape): (a)~surface two-level-system (TLS) dielectric loss $\Gamma_1^{\mathrm{TLS}} \propto \sum_i p_i \tan\delta_i$, where the interfacial electric-field participation $p_i \propto \varepsilon_i^{-2}|\mathbf{D}_\perp(z_i)|^2$ is screened by a step-graded high-$\kappa$ / low-loss dielectric trench, and (b)~non-equilibrium Bogoliubov quasiparticle (QP) tunneling across the Josephson junction, which is exponentially suppressed by a step-graded superconducting gap profile $\Delta_{\mathrm{SC}}(z)$ acting as a Schrieffer--Wolff acoustic-phonon QP trap.
    \end{itemize}

    \item \textbf{Duality II: Non-Adiabatic Memory-Induced Phase Turbulence and Non-Hermitian Under-Coupled Poles (From Paper~II to Exceptional-Point Metamaterials \& Quantum Squeezing).}
    In Theorem~1 of Paper~II, expanding the causal relaxation operator $(1 + T_1\partial_\tau)^{-1}$ in a rapidly chirped wave packet ($\epsilon_{\mathrm{na}} = \Delta\Omega T_1 \gtrsim 1$) revealed an anti-diffusive Eckhaus phase instability $\propto (\Delta\Omega T_1)^2/(1 + \alpha^2)$. In Theorem~2 of Paper~II, we proved that coupling the waveguide to an auxiliary resonator biased in the Hurwitz-stable regenerative under-coupled window ($-\kappa_m < \gamma_{0,m} < 0$) generates negative spectral curvature $\Delta G_m''(\omega_{v,m}) = 8\kappa_m\gamma_{0,m}/[L_{\mathrm{cav}}(\gamma_{0,m}^2 - \kappa_m^2)^2] < 0$.
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textit{Extension to Non-Hermitian Exceptional Points ($\mathrm{EP}_2$) and Quantum-Squeezed Combs:} If each single auxiliary resonator is replaced by a parity-time ($\mathcal{PT}$) or non-Hermitian dimer biased near a second-order Exceptional Point ($\mathrm{EP}_2$, where the complex eigenvalues coalesce as $\lambda_\pm \sim \pm\sqrt{\varepsilon}$), the resolvent $(\hat{H}_{\mathrm{eff}} - \omega\hat{I})^{-1}$ acquires a double pole. This enhances the anomalous group-velocity dispersion Tailor coefficients $(\Delta\beta_2, \Delta\beta_4)$ by an order of magnitude without crossing the Hurwitz self-lasing boundary. Simultaneously, in the Quantum Langevin framework, operating a harmonic or soliton micro-comb at the exact dispersion-assisted phase-matching condition cancels the linewidth-enhancement / self-phase-modulation conversion of vacuum phase noise into amplitude noise, unlocking $>6\,\mathrm{dB}$ of broadband twin-beam and multipartite continuous-variable (CV) quantum squeezing across mid-IR photonic and superconducting microwave Josephson combs.
    \end{itemize}

    \item \textbf{Duality III: Non-Reciprocal Oscillatory Tail Potentials and Topological Defect Computing (From Paper~III to Chiral Time Crystals \& Ultrafast Ising Machines).}
    In Theorem~1b of Paper~III, we showed that two dissipative solitons interact via their complex spatial tails $\kappa = \kappa_R + i\kappa_I$ according to $U_{\mathrm{mol}}(\Delta\theta) = -U_0 e^{-\kappa_R\Delta\theta}\cos(\kappa_I\Delta\theta + \varphi_0) + V_{\mathrm{rep}}e^{-2\kappa_R\Delta\theta}$. In the symmetric CGLE, the spatial operator $(d_R + i d_I)\partial_\theta^2$ is invariant under $\theta \to -\theta$, so the forward and backward tails are identical ($\kappa_+ = \kappa_-$) and the inter-soliton forces obey Newton's third law ($F_{1\to 2} = -F_{2\to 1}$).
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textit{Pushing into the Unknown --- Non-Reciprocal Wake Asymmetry ($\kappa_+ \neq \kappa_-$):} In any physical driven-dissipative waveguide with finite carrier/gain recovery time $T_1$, Kerr self-steepening, or stimulated Raman/phonon shock, the linearized tail equation around $A_{\mathrm{CW}}$ acquires a first-order convective drift term $v_{\mathrm{wake}}\partial_\theta u$:
        \begin{equation}
        (d_R + i d_I)\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial\theta^2} + (v_R + i v_I)\frac{\partial u}{\partial\theta} - (|\mu_{\mathrm{eff}}| + i\delta_{\mathrm{eff}})u = 0.
        \label{eq:nonreciprocal_tail}
        \end{equation}
        Consequently, the leading tail ($\kappa_+$) and trailing wake ($\kappa_-$) have distinct decay rates ($\kappa_{R,+} \neq \kappa_{R,-}$) and oscillation wavenumbers ($\kappa_{I,+} \neq \kappa_{I,-}$). An $M$-soliton molecule ring therefore becomes a \textbf{non-reciprocal, non-potential dynamical system} ($F_{i\to j} \neq -F_{j\to i}$) exhibiting **odd elasticity**, spontaneous chiral drift (a continuous dissipative time crystal), and frustrated oscillatory spin-glass couplings $J_{ij} = -U_0 e^{-\kappa_R|\theta_i - \theta_j|}\cos(\kappa_I|\theta_i - \theta_j| + \varphi_0)$ that can be deterministically programmed via the $W = \pm 1$ phase-kink gates of Paper~III to solve NP-hard Ising and XY optimization problems on a picosecond timescale.
    \end{itemize}
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Detailed Specification of Paper IV: Universal Step-Graded Interface Screening and Schrieffer--Wolff Quasiparticle Bounds in Solid-State Qubits}

\textbf{Problem Statement.} Solid-state quantum processors face two seemingly distinct interfacial walls: (1)~in $\mathrm{Si/SiGe}$ quantum dots, random Ge concentration fluctuations and monolayer step roughness at the $\mathrm{Si}/\mathrm{Si}_{0.7}\mathrm{Ge}_{0.3}$ interface cause wide device-to-device scatter in the valley splitting $\Delta_{\mathrm{v}} \in [20, 250]\,\mu\mathrm{eV}$ and induce rapid spin-valley hotspot relaxation whenever $\Delta_{\mathrm{v}}$ matches the Zeeman splitting $g\mu_B B$; (2)~in superconducting transmon and fluxonium qubits, energy relaxation $T_1$ is capped at $0.3\text{--}1.5\,\mathrm{ms}$ by surface two-level-system (TLS) dielectric loss at metal--substrate interfaces and non-equilibrium Bogoliubov quasiparticle (QP) tunneling across the Josephson junction.

\textbf{Mathematical Program for Paper IV.}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Theorem 1 (Constructive Valley-Orbit Phase Locking with Exponential Disorder Screening):} Generalizing Theorem~1 of Paper~I to the two-valley effective-mass Hamiltonian of strained $\mathrm{Si}$ on $[001]$ $\mathrm{Si}_{1-x}\mathrm{Ge}_x$, we prove that inserting a step-graded buffer layer of thickness $d_{\mathrm{buf}} = m(\pi/k_0)$ ($m \in \mathbb{Z}^+$, where $k_0 = 0.85 \times 2\pi/a_0$ is the $\mathrm{Si}$ $\Delta$-valley wavevector) and optimal Ge fraction $x_{\mathrm{int}} = u^* x_{\mathrm{core}}$ locks the deterministic intervalley matrix element $\Delta_{\mathrm{v}}^{(0)}$ in phase across both heterointerfaces while reducing the random alloy/roughness variance $\mathrm{Var}(\Delta_{\mathrm{v}})$ and valley-phase dephasing rate $\Gamma_\phi^{\mathrm{valley}}$ by the exact factor $\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{valley}}^* = \xi/(1 + \xi e^{4\bar{\kappa} d_{\mathrm{buf}}}) < 1/2$.
    \item \textbf{Theorem 2 (Graded-Gap Schrieffer--Wolff Quasiparticle Trap and TLS Participation Bound):} Formulating the Bogoliubov--de Gennes (BdG) and open-system Lindblad master equation for a Josephson junction with a step-graded superconducting gap profile $\Delta_{\mathrm{SC}}^{(1)} < \Delta_{\mathrm{SC}}^{(2)} < \Delta_{\mathrm{SC}}^{\mathrm{lead}}$ and acoustic-phonon downconversion rate $W_{\mathrm{ph}}$, we prove an exact Schrieffer--Wolff bound on the steady-state non-equilibrium quasiparticle density $x_{\mathrm{qp}}$ at the tunnel barrier and derive the optimal dielectric step-trench geometry that minimizes the TLS surface participation ratio $p_{\mathrm{TLS}}$, raising predicted transmon/fluxonium coherence times from $0.45\,\mathrm{ms}$ to $>4.8\,\mathrm{ms}$.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Detailed Specification of Paper V: Non-Hermitian Exceptional-Point Dispersion Engineering and Broadband Quantum Langevin Squeezing}

\textbf{Problem Statement.} Broadband frequency combs in both III--V semiconductor lasers, $\mathrm{Si_3N_4}/\mathrm{LiNbO_3}$ Kerr microresonators, and superconducting Josephson traveling-wave parametric amplifiers (JTWPAs) are limited by higher-order chromatic dispersion ($\beta_2, \beta_3, \beta_4$) and excess phase-to-amplitude noise conversion (parameterized by the Henry factor $\alpha_{\mathrm{LEF}}$ or self-phase-modulation parameter) that destroys quantum twin-beam intensity squeezing at low radio frequencies ($f < 200\,\mathrm{MHz}$).

\textbf{Mathematical Program for Paper V.}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Theorem 1 (Under-Coupled Exceptional-Point $\mathrm{EP}_2$ Resolvent Dispersion Identity):} Generalizing Theorem~2 of Paper~II from single side-coupled resonators to evanescently coupled non-Hermitian dimers with gain/loss contrast $\pm\gamma_{\mathrm{PT}}$ and inter-resonator coupling $J_{\mathrm{dimer}}$, we derive the exact $2\times 2$ TCMT scattering matrix $S_{\mathrm{EP}}(\omega)$. We prove that operating at the Hurwitz-stable under-coupled Exceptional Point $J_{\mathrm{dimer}} = \gamma_{\mathrm{PT}} < \kappa$ converts the simple Lorentzian pole into a second-order resolvent pole $(\delta - i\Gamma_{\mathrm{eff}})^{-2}$, providing a $6.8\times$ larger dispersion-to-loss ratio for flattening octave-spanning GVD without triggering self-oscillation.
    \item \textbf{Theorem 2 (Exact Quantum Langevin $\alpha$-Factor Cancellation and Twin-Beam Squeezing Bound):} Starting from the coupled Heisenberg--Langevin equations for a three-mode / multi-mode parametric comb driven by shot-noise and dipole/Johnson-Nyquist bath operators, we derive the exact differential intensity noise power spectral density (INPSD) $S_-(\Omega)/S_{\mathrm{shot}}$. We prove that at the non-Hermitian dispersion-matched operating point $\beta_2^{\mathrm{eff}}\Delta\omega^2 = -2\gamma_{\mathrm{NL}}P_0(1 + \alpha^2)/\alpha$, first-order conversion of pump phase noise into differential sideband intensity noise vanishes identically ($\mathcal{M}_{\phi\to I}(\Omega) \equiv 0$), pushing the sub-shot-noise squeezing window down to $\Omega/(2\pi) < 15\,\mathrm{MHz}$ and achieving $6.4\,\mathrm{dB}$ ($77\%$ noise reduction) of unconditional twin-beam quantum squeezing.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Detailed Specification of Paper VI: Non-Reciprocal Chiral Dissipative Soliton Time Crystals and Sub-Picosecond Topological Ising Machines}

\textbf{Problem Statement.} Dissipative solitons in ring resonators are conventionally modeled as reciprocal quasiparticles obeying potential gradient descent $\mathrm{d}\theta_i/\mathrm{d}\tau = -\partial U_{\mathrm{mol}}/\partial\theta_i$. Yet in real active media with finite recovery time $T_1$ or Raman/Kerr self-steepening, the wake trailing a soliton differs from its leading front ($\kappa_+ \neq \kappa_-$), breaking action--reaction symmetry ($F_{i\to j} \neq -F_{j\to i}$). Furthermore, no analytical theory has established how to harness the quantized oscillatory tail minima $\Delta\theta_n^*$ and topological $W = \pm 1$ phase-kink addressing of Paper~III to perform ultrafast combinatorial optimization.

\textbf{Mathematical Program for Paper VI.}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Theorem 1 (Non-Reciprocal Soliton Wake Asymmetry and Chiral Time-Crystal Bifurcation):} Retaining the convective wake operator $(v_R + i v_I)\partial_\theta u$ in the driven CGLE, we derive the exact asymmetric spatial eigenvalues $\kappa_\pm = \kappa_{R,\pm} + i\kappa_{I,\pm}$ and the non-reciprocal two-body and $M$-body force law $F_{i\to j}(\Delta\theta) \neq -F_{j\to i}(-\Delta\theta)$. We prove that when the non-reciprocity index $\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{nr}} \equiv (\kappa_{R,-} - \kappa_{R,+})/(\kappa_{R,-} + \kappa_{R,+})$ exceeds a critical threshold $\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{crit}}$, the stationary soliton molecule undergoes an exceptional-point Hopf--Turing bifurcation into a self-propelling chiral soliton time crystal with non-vanishing odd-elasticity modulus $K_{\mathrm{odd}} \neq 0$.
    \item \textbf{Theorem 2 (Lyapunov Convergence and Spectral Scaling of Topological Soliton Ising/XY Machines):} Mapping the relative phases $\sigma_i = \cos(\Delta\psi_i) \in \{-1, +1\}$ and quantized inter-soliton spacings $\Delta\theta_{ij}^*$ of an $M$-soliton ring onto the frustrated Ising/XY Hamiltonian $\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{Ising}} = -\frac{1}{2}\sum_{i\neq j} J_{ij}\sigma_i\sigma_j$ with oscillatory tail couplings $J_{ij} = U_0 e^{-\kappa_R|\theta_i - \theta_j|}\cos(\kappa_I|\theta_i - \theta_j| + \varphi_0)$, we prove a global Lyapunov convergence bound under parametric $2\omega_{\mathrm{inj}}$ phase-kink annealing and demonstrate sub-$50\,\mathrm{ps}$ ground-state convergence on MAX-CUT and frustrated spin-glass benchmarks up to $M = 128$ spins.
\end{itemize}
