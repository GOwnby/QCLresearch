## Executive Overview: Three-Phase Architecture (Papers I–IX)

Our research program is structured into three cumulative phases:
- **Phase I (Papers I–III — Compiled & Verified, Zenodo/arXiv Ready):** Resolves three foundational limits in semiconductor quantum cascade lasers (QCLs)—the $261\,\mathrm{K}$ THz operating temperature ceiling, non-adiabatic extendon phase turbulence in octave-spanning heterogeneous combs, and deterministic topological phase-kink addressing of sub-picosecond soliton molecules.
- **Phase II (Papers IV–VI — Compiled & Verified, Zenodo/arXiv Ready):** Extracts the three universal operator-theoretic dualities discovered in Phase I and proves their exact counterparts across solid-state quantum computing ($\mathrm{Si/SiGe}$ spin-valley qubits and $\mathrm{NbTiN/TiN/Al}$ superconducting transmons/fluxoniums), non-Hermitian Exceptional-Point ($\mathrm{EP}_2$) quantum optics and Josephson metamaterials, and non-reciprocal chiral soliton time crystals and topological Ising machines.
- **Phase III (Papers VII–IX — Pushing into the Gap of the Unknown):** Intersects the proved theorems of Papers IV, V, and VI to open three new theoretical frontiers in general quantum physics:
  1. **Paper VII (Paper IV $\times$ Paper V):** *Acoustic-Metamaterial-Shielded Gottesman–Kitaev–Preskill (GKP) and Kerr-Cat Bosonic Quantum Error Correction Below the Fault-Tolerant Threshold: Suppressing Correlated Phonon-Burst Errors via Fröhlich–Piezoelectric Impedance Cloaking*
  2. **Paper VIII (Paper V $\times$ Paper VI):** *Quantum-Squeezed Non-Reciprocal Soliton Engines: Breaking the Classical Thermodynamic Uncertainty Relation via Exceptional-Point Odd Elasticity and Non-Hermitian Berry Curvature*
  3. **Paper IX (Paper IV $\times$ Paper V $\times$ Paper VI):** *Non-Abelian Chiral Soliton Braiding and Squeezed-Instanton Topological Spin-Glass Machines: Sub-Picosecond Quantum Tunneling Through Ultrametric Spin-Glass Barriers*
